# Core vocabulary

This file gives the core words of this skill.
Each line gives one word, its part of speech, and its permitted forms.

This vocabulary is our own selection for IT, data, and AI text.
It uses the principles of ASD-STE100: a small set of words, and one meaning for each word.
It is not the official STE dictionary.
If your project uses the official dictionary, the official dictionary has priority.

Format: `WORD (part of speech) [permitted forms]`

Parts of speech: v = verb, n = noun, adj = adjective, adv = adverb, prep = preposition, conj = conjunction, pron = pronoun, art = article.

Rules for this list:

- Use a word only as the part of speech that this list gives (rule W2).
- Use only the verb forms that this list gives (rule W4). There are no "-ing" forms.
- The plural of a noun is also permitted.
- A word that is not in this list is permitted only as a technical name or a technical verb (rule W1).
- The technical names are in the files in `references/terms/`. The technical verbs are in `references/technical-verbs.md`.
- Some words have a narrow meaning. The list of these words is at the end of this file.

## Words with a narrow meaning

Use these words only with the meaning that this list gives.

- **ANSWER** (n, v): the text that replies to a question. A model "returns an answer". Do not write that a model "knows" the answer.
- **APPROVE** (v): a person or an approval gate gives permission for an action.
- **BACK** (adv): to a previous step or position. Write "go back to step 3".
- **BE** (v): use "is", "are", "was", "were". Do not use "been" or "being".
- **CAN** (v): ability or possibility. Use "could" only as the past tense of "can".
- **CHECK** (n): a noun only. Write "do a check of the logs" or "examine the logs", not "check the logs".
- **CONTACT** (v): send a message to a person or a team, or speak to them.
- **FAIL** (v): a system, a job, a request, or a test does not complete correctly.
- **FOLLOW** (v): come after. Write "the step that follows". To mean "do what a rule says", use "obey".
- **HAVE** (v): possession only. Do not use "has", "have", or "had" as a helping verb.
- **KNOW** (v): for persons only. Do not use it for models or agents.
- **LIMIT** (n, v): a maximum or a minimum value. Write "limit the result to 100 rows".
- **MUST** (v): a requirement. Use "must not" for a prohibition.
- **OPEN** (v, adj): open a file, a page, a port, or a connection.
- **POINT** (v): show a direction. In DNS, write "the record resolves to", not "points to".
- **RUN**: a technical verb. Refer to `technical-verbs.md`.
- **SEE** (v): with your eyes only. Write "make sure that", not "see if". Write "refer to", not "see the guide".
- **SET** (v): give a value to a parameter or a property. Write "set the timeout to 30 s".
- **SIZE** (n): the quantity of data, or the dimension of an object or a resource.
- **STATE** (n): the condition of a system at a given time. Write "the state of the cluster".
- **TEST** (n): a noun only. Write "do a test of the pipeline", not "test the pipeline".
- **TURN** (v): move around an axis. To mean "change to", write "become" or "change to".
- **WILL** (v): the future. Do not use it for a requirement. Use "must".
